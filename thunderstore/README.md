# Hearthline

![Hearthline](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/BlackHearthx/Hearthline@main/icon.png)

Raise a stronger line. Favorites feed the stars. Steal a wild cub — or carry your herd home.

**Stars, favorites, wild steal, and yard tools in one mod** — do not stack with BreedingUpgrades or Procreation Plus.

By **BlackHearthx**.

---

## Stars

Offspring and eggs can be born **one ★ above** the parent.  
Default chance **5%**; with a **favorite meal** (while still fed) **25%**. Cap is **2★** (higher needs [CLLC](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/Smoothbrain/CreatureLevelAndLootControl/)).

![1. Stars and hover](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/BlackHearthx/Hearthline@main/docs/tutorial/tutorial_01_hover.png)

## Yard

Hover on livestock shows:

- **Bond** — love progress toward breeding  
- **Expecting** — countdown to birth  
- **Pen full** — no more breeding in range  
- **Growing** — cub maturity  
- Favorite-meal tip when it applies  

**Mate draw:** fed, calm adults walk toward a nearby partner of the same species (not when hungry, alert, pregnant, young, or the pen is full).

## Steal & carry

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **Z** | Steal wild young, or pick up / put down young and tamed adults |

Wild steal costs stamina, has a cooldown, and can aggro nearby wild adults of the same species.  
Damage while carrying drops the animal. You cannot attack while carrying.

![3. Steal and carry](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/BlackHearthx/Hearthline@main/docs/tutorial/tutorial_03_steal_carry.png)

## Wild herds

Wild adults can breed; babies stay wild. A soft pregnancy chance and occasional “already has a cub” spawn keep the woods from flooding.

![4. Wild herds](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/BlackHearthx/Hearthline@main/docs/tutorial/tutorial_04_wild_herd.png)

## Como usar (PT-BR)

**Estrelas** — filhotes (e ovos) podem nascer com **+1★**. Chance base **5%**; com **comida favorita** (ainda alimentado) **25%**. Teto **2★** (acima disso precisa de CLLC).

| Animal | Favorita |
| --- | --- |
| Javali | Cenoura |
| Lobo | Carne crua |
| Lox | Cevada |
| Galinha | Cevada |
| Asksvin | Vinhaberry |
| Alce | Lingonberry |

**Curral** — no hover: vínculo, gravidez com contagem, curral cheio, crescimento da cria, dica de favorita. Adultos calmos e alimentados andam até o parceiro (**mate draw**).

**Z** — rouba filhote selvagem ou carrega cria / adulto domado (custa stamina, tem cooldown, pode acordar a manada).

**Manadas selvagens** — selvagens podem procriar; filhote nasce selvagem. Chance extra evita explosão na floresta.

Não use junto com BreedingUpgrades ou Procreation Plus.
